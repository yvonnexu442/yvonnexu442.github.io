03 Review approach

A disciplined approach to useful review.

These principles describe Yvonne’s own review approach—not an official conference rubric.

  1. 01

    Methodological rigor

    Assess whether claims are supported by appropriate methods and evidence.

  2. 02

    Experimental validity

    Examine whether evaluation design and reported results support the stated contribution.

  3. 03

    Interpretability

    Identify assumptions, limitations, and reasoning that should be made more legible.

  4. 04

    Constructive feedback

    Offer specific observations that can help authors strengthen the work.

  5. 05

    Operational relevance

    Ask whether the contribution supports a meaningful analytical or operational decision.
