Technical report · 2019

A Multi-asset Transportation Infrastructure Asset Management Framework and Modeling for Local Governments

An early public output examining transportation infrastructure asset management through a multi-asset framework and mathematical modeling for local governments.

Abstract

Local governments manage substantial roadway and bridge networks while facing aging assets and constrained budgets. The report develops a systematic, multi-asset framework for cost-effective maintenance, rehabilitation, and reconstruction decisions, with the aim of improving budget allocation and accountability.

Authors
Zhaohua Wang, Ryan Salameh, Caitlin Mildner, Mingshu Li, Yihua Xu
