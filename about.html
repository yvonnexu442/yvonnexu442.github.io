01 About

Yihua (Yvonne) Xu

Yihua Xu is the legal and publication name. Yvonne is the preferred professional name.

Applied research shaped by operational questions.

Yihua Xu is an applied operations researcher and Data & AI professional whose work centers on the systems surrounding analytical decisions: the evidence available, the constraints shaping execution, and the conditions determining whether a capability creates value.

Her Operational Decision Systems research program grew from repeated experience with planning, inventory, reverse logistics, analytical governance, and implementation.

02 What I care about

Better models matter only when they support better decisions.

I am interested in questions that appear repeatedly in operational systems but cannot be answered by predictive performance alone.

  • What evidence was actually available when a decision was made?
  • Can a recommendation survive changing capacity, policy, and execution constraints?
  • How should incomplete information change action?

These questions connect my professional practice and research.

03 How I work

Structured inquiry, practical constraints, and careful implementation.

  1. 01

    Start with the decision

    I define the action, owner, timing, and consequence before selecting a method.

  2. 02

    Make constraints visible

    I treat capacity, policy, workflow, evidence timing, and human judgment as part of the system.

  3. 03

    Connect analysis to use

    I ask whether the work can be interpreted, reviewed, implemented, and maintained.

  4. 04

    Preserve uncertainty

    I avoid presenting incomplete evidence as more certain than it is.
